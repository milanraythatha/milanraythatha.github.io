About

Finding the pattern nobody else sees. That's the challenge I'm after.

Most of my work starts the same way: several exports that don't agree with each other and a question someone senior needs answered. I like that part. I clean and join the data, work out what it actually says, and build the report or dashboard so the answer keeps arriving without me.

Right now that is procurement and spend data at Morrison Healthcare. Before that it was institutional research and funding data at Greenville Technical College, customer and call data at HashTek, and research data at UT Arlington. I'm also working on a Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence.

Off the clock: looking for a great cup of coffee, or a hiking trail I haven't done yet.
